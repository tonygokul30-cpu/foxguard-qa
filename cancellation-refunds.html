Cancellation and Refunds

Updated 29 October 2026

Cancel anytime

You can cancel your FoxGuard QA subscription anytime by emailing tonygokul30@gmail.com from your subscription email address. Include your website URL and Razorpay subscription or payment reference so we can identify the subscription. We confirm cancellation by email and stop future renewals once cancellation is processed.

Cancellation does not automatically refund payments already made. Any remaining service period and eligible refund will be confirmed with you. Contact us before your next renewal to allow time for processing.

Refund requests

To request a refund, email the same support address with your payment reference and reason. We review eligibility based on the service agreed and delivered, billing errors and applicable consumer rights, and communicate the outcome by email.

Eligible refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after approval through the original payment method. The time taken for the credit to appear may also depend on your bank or payment provider. No physical return is required because FoxGuard QA is a digital service.

Questions and billing issues

Contact us promptly if you believe you were charged incorrectly or did not receive the agreed service. Our operating address and support hours are listed on the Contact Us page.
