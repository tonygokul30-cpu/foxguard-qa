Shipping Policy

Updated 30 October 2026

100% digital delivery

FoxGuard QA is a 100% digital cloud SaaS service. Website audit reports and service communications are delivered electronically via email to the address supplied during onboarding.

No physical products are shipped. There are no couriers, physical delivery charges or shipping addresses required for service delivery.

When delivery begins

After subscription confirmation, daily public-page monitoring starts automatically for the website and email submitted with testing permission. Reports are emailed after checks complete. Additional custom flows or access require configuration; queue and email-provider delays can affect delivery.

Daily monitoring uses 06:00 AM India time unless another schedule is agreed. Scheduled runs may be delayed by the hosting platform. The “2-minute” free audit refers to the request process, not a guaranteed report delivery time.

Missing reports

Please check your spam folder and contact tonygokul30@gmail.com if an expected report has not arrived. Include your website URL and subscription or payment reference, never card details.
