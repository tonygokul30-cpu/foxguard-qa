Our service

FoxGuard QA, operated by Gokul Gopalakrishnan, provides digital website testing and monitoring. We test agreed pages and customer journeys and deliver reports electronically. You must own or have explicit permission to test every submitted website and provide appropriate test accounts where needed.

Subscriptions and payment

Monthly subscriptions are paid through Razorpay. Review the amount, currency, taxes, billing interval and recurring-payment authorization displayed at checkout before paying. The Founding offer applies to the first five eligible clients and continues while the qualifying subscription remains active. Contact us to confirm availability or scope before purchase.

Enter your website URL and email and confirm testing permission before checkout. Subscription confirmation automatically activates daily public-page monitoring using the same email. Coverage and limitations

Reports describe the checks actually performed at a particular time. They do not guarantee a bug-free website, uninterrupted service or detection of every issue. Self-healing refers to approved test-selector repairs, not changes to your application code. Custom flows and additional schedules require agreed configuration.
